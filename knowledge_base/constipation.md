# Constipation in adults
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/constipation/
Reviewed-on: 2026-10-09
Scope: General information; source-linked editorial summary, not clinician-reviewed or diagnostic.

## Assessment, self-care and escalation
Ask about duration, stool pattern, pain, bleeding, medicines and pregnancy. For uncomplicated constipation, gradually add fibre from vegetables, fruit and whole grains, drink fluids and remain active. Allow time for a regular toilet routine and do not ignore the urge. A pharmacist can advise on a short course of a suitable laxative if these measures fail.

Precautions and escalation: See a clinician for persistent or recurrent symptoms, blood in stool, unexplained weight loss or a sudden bowel-habit change. Do not stop prescribed medicines independently. Children need age-specific advice.
Evidence: Public-health patient guidance. Apply only when symptoms fit; ask questions when unclear. Medicines require suitability checks and local pharmacist/clinician guidance. Do not invent a diagnosis or a traditional-system cure.
