# Heartburn and acid reflux
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heartburn-and-acid-reflux/
Reviewed-on: 2026-10-09
Scope: General information; source-linked editorial summary, not clinician-reviewed or diagnostic.

## Assessment, self-care and escalation
Ask about relationship to meals, duration, swallowing difficulty, vomiting and medicines. For familiar mild reflux, try smaller meals, avoid personal food triggers and avoid eating for three to four hours before bed. Ask a pharmacist whether short-term antacids or alginates are suitable; check interactions and pregnancy precautions.

Precautions and escalation: Do not assume chest pain is acidity: emergency safety rules take priority. Seek a clinician for symptoms on most days, food sticking, repeated vomiting or unexplained weight loss. Do not keep taking symptom-relief medicines long term without review.
Evidence: Public-health patient guidance. Apply only when symptoms fit; ask questions when unclear. Medicines require suitability checks and local pharmacist/clinician guidance. Do not invent a diagnosis or a traditional-system cure.
