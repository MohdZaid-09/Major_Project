# Dehydration
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dehydration/
Reviewed-on: 2026-10-09
Scope: General information; source-linked editorial summary, not clinician-reviewed or diagnostic.

## Assessment, self-care and escalation
Ask about fluid loss, heat exposure, urine output, age and ability to drink. Thirst, dry mouth, dark urine and dizziness may suggest fluid loss but are not diagnostic. Take small sips and increase gradually. A pharmacist can advise on ORS for diarrhoea or vomiting. Infants and older adults need earlier assessment.

Precautions and escalation: Persistent dizziness on standing, reduced urine, unusual drowsiness or rapid breathing require urgent assessment. Confusion, difficulty waking or breathing difficulty needs emergency help. Fluid-restricted patients should seek individual advice.
Evidence: Public-health patient guidance. Apply only when symptoms fit; ask questions when unclear. Medicines require suitability checks and local pharmacist/clinician guidance. Do not invent a diagnosis or a traditional-system cure.
