# Diarrhoea and vomiting
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/diarrhoea-and-vomiting/
Reviewed-on: 2026-10-09
Scope: General information; source-linked editorial summary, not clinician-reviewed or diagnostic.

## Assessment, self-care and escalation
Ask about age, duration, frequency, blood, fever, abdominal pain, ability to keep fluids down and urine output. For mild symptoms, rest and take small frequent sips. A pharmacist can advise on oral rehydration solution (ORS); mix packaged ORS exactly as directed using safe water. Continue breastfeeding and normal-strength formula. Eat as tolerated. Wash hands with soap and avoid preparing food for others. Do not self-start antibiotics.

Precautions and escalation: Obtain urgent assessment for bloody diarrhoea, repeated vomiting that prevents drinking, dehydration, or an unwell infant. Vomiting blood, green vomit, confusion or sudden severe abdominal pain needs emergency care. Seek medical review if vomiting lasts over two days or diarrhoea over seven days; earlier if worsening.
Evidence: Public-health patient guidance. Apply only when symptoms fit; ask questions when unclear. Medicines require suitability checks and local pharmacist/clinician guidance. Do not invent a diagnosis or a traditional-system cure.
