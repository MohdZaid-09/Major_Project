# Minor insect bites and stings
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/insect-bites-and-stings/
Reviewed-on: 2026-10-09
Scope: General information; source-linked editorial summary, not clinician-reviewed or diagnostic.

## Assessment, self-care and escalation
Ask about the insect, timing, location, swelling and previous allergic reactions. Wash a minor bite with soap and water, use a wrapped cold pack for swelling and avoid scratching. Ask a pharmacist about suitable itch relief.

Precautions and escalation: Lip, tongue or throat swelling, breathing difficulty or collapse needs emergency help. Bites in the mouth or near the eye, spreading redness, pus, fever or worsening pain need medical review. Persistent fever following bites needs assessment rather than assuming a simple local reaction.
Evidence: Public-health patient guidance. Apply only when symptoms fit; ask questions when unclear. Medicines require suitability checks and local pharmacist/clinician guidance. Do not invent a diagnosis or a traditional-system cure.
