# Heat exhaustion and heatstroke
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heat-exhaustion-heatstroke/
Reviewed-on: 2026-10-09
Scope: General information; source-linked editorial summary, not clinician-reviewed or diagnostic.

## Assessment, self-care and escalation
Ask about heat exposure, alertness, temperature and ability to drink. Move an alert person with heat-related fatigue or dizziness to a cool place, remove unnecessary clothing, cool the skin with water and fanning, and offer water or rehydration drink. Stay with them.

Precautions and escalation: Confusion, seizures, unconsciousness, very high temperature or continued illness after 30 minutes of cooling needs immediate emergency help. Continue cooling while help arrives. Do not offer oral fluids to someone who cannot safely swallow. Do not substitute herbal remedies for cooling and emergency care.
Evidence: Public-health patient guidance. Apply only when symptoms fit; ask questions when unclear. Medicines require suitability checks and local pharmacist/clinician guidance. Do not invent a diagnosis or a traditional-system cure.
