# Minor sprains and muscle strains
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/sprains-and-strains/
Reviewed-on: 2026-10-09
Scope: General information; source-linked editorial summary, not clinician-reviewed or diagnostic.

## Assessment, self-care and escalation
Ask how the injury happened, where it hurts, ability to bear weight and presence of swelling or numbness. Initially protect the injury, pause painful activity, elevate it and apply a cloth-wrapped cold pack for up to 20 minutes at a time. Avoid early heat and massage. Begin gentle movement when pain allows; return to sport gradually.

Precautions and escalation: Inability to walk a few steps, increasing pain or marked swelling needs assessment. Deformity, numbness or a cold or blue limb requires emergency evaluation. Do not assume a fracture is only a sprain.
Evidence: Public-health patient guidance. Apply only when symptoms fit; ask questions when unclear. Medicines require suitability checks and local pharmacist/clinician guidance. Do not invent a diagnosis or a traditional-system cure.
