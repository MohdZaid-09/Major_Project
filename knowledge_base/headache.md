# Headache

## Allopathic (Modern Medicine)
Most headaches are tension-type headaches, caused by muscle tension, stress, dehydration, poor posture, or eye strain, and are not dangerous. Migraines are a distinct type involving throbbing pain, often with nausea or light sensitivity. First-line care includes rest in a quiet dark room, hydration, and OTC pain relievers (like paracetamol or ibuprofen) as directed on packaging. A doctor should be seen urgently for a "worst headache of life," headache with fever and stiff neck, headache after a head injury, or headache with vision changes, confusion, or weakness (these can indicate a serious underlying condition).
Evidence Level: strong
Precautions: Avoid overusing OTC painkillers, as frequent use can itself cause "rebound headaches." Seek immediate care for any of the red-flag symptoms listed above.

## Ayurvedic
Ayurveda often classifies headache (Shirahshoola) by dosha imbalance — for example, Vata-type (throbbing, associated with stress/anxiety), Pitta-type (burning, associated with heat/anger), or Kapha-type (dull, heavy, associated with congestion). Traditional approaches include Shirodhara (oil poured on the forehead), gentle head massage with medicated oils, and dietary adjustments suited to the dominant dosha.
Evidence Level: traditional/anecdotal
Precautions: Persistent or severe headaches should be medically evaluated rather than managed solely through traditional therapies, particularly to rule out serious causes.

## Unani
In Unani medicine, headache (Suda) is often attributed to abnormal Akhlat affecting the brain's temperament, or to external factors like exposure to heat/cold or digestive disturbance. Management may include Ilaj-bil-Tadbir (rest, controlled environment), dietary modification, and topical oil applications, guided by a Hakim's assessment of the patient's individual temperament (Mizaj).
Evidence Level: traditional/anecdotal
Precautions: Chronic or severe headaches warrant proper diagnostic evaluation; traditional remedies should complement, not replace, medical assessment when red-flag symptoms are present.

## Homeopathic
Homeopathic remedy selection for headache is based on specific characteristics of the pain and accompanying symptoms (e.g. Belladonna for throbbing headache with flushed face, Nux Vomica for headache linked to overindulgence or stress). As with other conditions, high-quality clinical evidence for effectiveness beyond placebo is limited.
Evidence Level: limited
Precautions: Do not rely on homeopathic treatment alone if experiencing any red-flag headache symptoms (sudden severe onset, head injury, neurological symptoms) — seek emergency medical care instead.
