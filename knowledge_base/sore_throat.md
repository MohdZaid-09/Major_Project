# Sore throat
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/sore-throat/
Reviewed-on: 2026-10-09
Scope: General information; source-linked editorial summary, not clinician-reviewed or diagnostic.

## Assessment, self-care and escalation
Ask about duration, fever, swallowing and breathing. For mild symptoms, drink fluids, rest, avoid smoke and try soft foods. Adults may gargle warm salt water and spit it out; children should not try this. A pharmacist can advise on suitable pain relief after checking age, pregnancy and other medicines. Antibiotics are not routinely needed; do not self-prescribe.

Precautions and escalation: Breathing difficulty, inability to swallow, drooling or rapidly worsening severe symptoms require emergency care. Seek review for dehydration, an immunocompromised person, a young child or symptoms not improving after a week.
Evidence: Public-health patient guidance. Apply only when symptoms fit; ask questions when unclear. Medicines require suitability checks and local pharmacist/clinician guidance. Do not invent a diagnosis or a traditional-system cure.
